Underwater Acoustics Risk
Thermocline-depth read on sonar shadow-zone risk — relevant to underwater surveillance / ASW planning
Paste one "lat, lon, YYYY-MM-DD" point per line, or use the sample points. A shallow thermocline bends sonar rays into a shadow zone; a deep, gradual one gives more uniform detection range.
